# Hackathon Colsubsidio × 30X — Logistics

Source: <https://innovacion.colsubsidio.com/> (fetched 2026-07-22) + <https://www.youtube.com/@30XDevs/streams>.

## Key facts

- **Dates:** July 22–26, 2026 (5 days). Wed–Thu online; Fri–Sun hybrid (in person at Club La Colina Colsubsidio, Bogotá + online from all LATAM; 100 in-person spots).
- **Teams:** 3–5 people.
- **Four retos, ONE podium:** judges pick three absolute winners across ALL retos — we're not just competing against other reto-4 teams.
- **Prizes:** 1st $10,000,000 COP + 30X program seat per person + GitHub Copilot credits + implementation with Colsubsidio · 2nd $6,000,000 COP + AI mentorship · 3rd $3,000,000 COP + 30X discount.
- **After the podium:** winning solutions go to evaluation with Colsubsidio's product team for real implementation. (Judges likely weigh implementability, not just wow.)
- **Jurados:** experts in salud, crédito, innovación y tecnología from Colsubsidio + 30X — names "próximamente" at fetch time.
- **In-person perk list:** fast internet, workstations, meals, **mentorship rooms (technical + business)** — mentorías exist; use them.
