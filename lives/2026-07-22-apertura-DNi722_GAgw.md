## Signals & reading between the lines

- **Working software beats a polished pitch.** Repeated emphasis that judges must be able to *use* the live link to believe the solution works ("hasta que no lo usemos... no es tan real"), and that AI can be used for literally everything including the pitch video itself — what's scored is output quality, not effort purity or how it was made.
- **Scalability/repeatability at Col Subsidio's scale is an explicit judging lens**, stated via the "humanoid robot" example: a flashy, non-repeatable one-off (however technically impressive) is explicitly called out as a weak submission because it can't be rolled out across all locations. For Reto 4 this reads directly as: **favor a software/voice-agent approach that's cheap and replicable across every bodega**, not a bespoke or hardware-heavy solution.
- **Hardware is tolerated but structurally disadvantaged** in this format, because the judges (including the remote prefilter panel) cannot interact with it — a second, independent signal reinforcing "keep it software/interface-based" for a hardware-adjacent reto like inventory capture.
- **"Smart use of AI" is graded, not just "AI was used."** Innovation criterion explicitly separates genuine originality/utility from checkbox AI usage.
- **The organizers want a real path to production**, repeatedly framing the "biggest opportunity" as the solution reaching production with Col Subsidio and touching millions of affiliated users — this is a recruiting/business-development exercise for Col Subsidio as much as a hackathon, and the generous, non-extractive IP terms (explicitly contrasted with "other hackathons take it directly from you") appear designed to attract serious, reusable submissions rather than throwaway prototypes.
- **Team composition is enforced (3–5 people)**, and the organizers frame this as intentional — encouraging genuine multi-skill teams (backend/frontend/marketing examples given) rather than solo builders, reinforcing that a "complete package" (working demo + clean pitch + credible team) is valued over a narrow technical flex.
- **Judges can't be lobbied** — jury names are deliberately withheld until Friday/Saturday specifically to prevent participants contacting them, suggesting organizers take submission integrity seriously.
- Andrea (Col Subsidio) reused the phrase "quitarle esa fricción y el error al momento de digitalizar" for the hotelería reto — the emphasis on *reducing friction* as much as *reducing error* suggests speed/ease-of-capture (not just accuracy) is part of how they'll judge a winning Reto 4 solution.
